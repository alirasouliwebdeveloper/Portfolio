OVERVIEW

From problem to launch

The challenge

The old store was slow on mobile, checkout had four steps too many, and managing stock meant editing spreadsheets by hand.

The solution

A Laravel API with a Filament admin panel, and a statically generated Next.js storefront with a two-step checkout.

The result

Pages load in under a second, more visitors finish checkout, and the team updates products without calling a developer.

WHAT I BUILT

Key features

Online payments
